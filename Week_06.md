# Week 06

## Clock / Time

This sketch approaches the idea of a clock as a system that marks the passage of time rather than measuring it in a conventional way. Time is generated through a continuous physical process: a ball moving back and forth across the screen. Each collision with the sides represents a unit of time passing, turning motion into a temporal reference. Instead of relying on hands, numbers, or a static interface, the sketch uses repetition, rhythm, and accumulation to make time perceptible. The viewer does not read the time directly but experiences it through movement and change, emphasizing duration and continuity over precise timekeeping
