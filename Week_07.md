### My sketch

This sketch generates a simple, modular face by combining basic geometric shapes through a system of interchangeable parts. The face is built from a main head shape, onto which hair, ears, eyes, nose, and mouth are added, each selected from a small set of predefined variations. All elements are positioned relative to the center of the canvas and scaled according to the face dimensions to keep the proportions consistent. On each mouse click, the components are randomized, producing a new face and demonstrating how recognizable forms can emerge from simple rules and minimal shapes.
